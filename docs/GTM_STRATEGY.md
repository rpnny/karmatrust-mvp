# Go-to-Market Strategy

**Project**: KarmaTrust - Credit Infrastructure for TradFi & DeFi  
**Stage**: Post-Hackathon Planning  
**Last Updated**: 2026-02-04  

---

## Executive Summary

KarmaTrust's GTM strategy focuses on establishing credibility in the developer community first, then expanding to protocol integrations, and finally targeting enterprise customers (banks, fintechs).

**Core Message**: "We're the credit bureau for blockchain - infrastructure that both TradFi and DeFi trust."

---

## Target Market Segments

### Segment 1: DeFi Protocols (Primary - Short-term)

**Who**:
- Lending protocols (Aave, Compound, Morpho)
- DEXs with lending features
- Stablecoin protocols (MakerDAO, Frax)

**Pain Point**:
- Need credit assessment for undercollateralized lending
- Current solutions lack privacy and verifiability
- Institutions require familiar metrics (FICO-style)

**Value Proposition**:
> "Enable undercollateralized lending with verifiable, privacy-preserving credit scores."

**Entry Strategy**:
1. Open-source SDK for easy integration
2. Free tier for small protocols (<$10M TVL)
3. Case study with one mid-tier protocol

---

### Segment 2: Traditional Finance (Secondary - Medium-term)

**Who**:
- Crypto-curious banks (JPMorgan, HSBC, DBS)
- Credit rating agencies (exploring blockchain)
- Neobanks (Revolut, N26 crypto divisions)

**Pain Point**:
- Want to enter DeFi but lack risk assessment tools
- Need familiar metrics (FICO) backed by blockchain data
- Regulatory concerns about opaque scoring

**Value Proposition**:
> "FICO-equivalent scores with blockchain transparency and cryptographic verification."

**Entry Strategy**:
1. Pilot program with 1-2 forward-thinking banks
2. White-label solution for internal use
3. Regulatory compliance documentation

---

### Segment 3: Fintech & Web3 Companies (Tertiary - Long-term)

**Who**:
- Crypto card issuers (Crypto.com, Coinbase Card)
- Web2.5 lending platforms
- GameFi with economic systems

**Pain Point**:
- Need credit limits for card products
- Want on-chain reputation without building infrastructure
- GameFi needs portable credit across games

**Value Proposition**:
> "Plug-and-play credit infrastructure with ZK privacy."

**Entry Strategy**:
1. API-first product with clear pricing
2. Partner program for integrators
3. Revenue share model for high-volume users

## Competitive Positioning

### Positioning Statement

> "For DeFi protocols and traditional financial institutions who need verifiable credit assessment, KarmaTrust provides the first privacy-preserving credit infrastructure. Unlike static scoring solutions, our VCSM technology offers cryptographically verifiable credit state evolution with real zero-knowledge proofs."

### Key Differentiators

| Us | Them |
|----|------|
| State machine (VCSM) | Static snapshot |
| Real ZK proofs | Simulated or none |
| Infrastructure | Protocol/app |
| TradFi + DeFi | DeFi only |

## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| ZK circuit bugs | Formal verification, security audit |
| Scalability issues | Load testing, caching (already done) |
| Data source reliability | Multi-source fallback (Etherscan, RPC) |

### Market Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Competitor copies VCSM | Patent/publish first, build moat |
| DeFi market downturn | TradFi pivot (dual market) |
| Regulatory changes | Compliance-first design |

### Execution Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Slow adoption | Free tier, integration support |
| Team bandwidth | Prioritize high-impact tasks |
| Funding gap | Revenue focus, bootstrap mindset |
